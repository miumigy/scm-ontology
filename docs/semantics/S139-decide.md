# S139 — Decide

S139 defines the SCM OS Decide semantic: selecting or authorizing a course of action from available alternatives under objectives, constraints, evidence, uncertainty, policy, and authority.

## Decide contract

```text
Observe → Diagnose → Plan
                 ↓
        Alternatives / Evaluation
                 ↓
       Evidence + Constraints
                 ↓
              Decision
                 ↓
          Plan / Action
```

A Decision is an intentional selection, authorization, or determination by an actor or decision authority. It is not a Recommendation, Plan, Action, or Outcome.

## Core boundaries

- Recommendation ≠ Decision
- Decision ≠ Plan
- Decision ≠ Action
- Decision ≠ Outcome
- Evaluation ≠ Decision
- Selected alternative ≠ Executed action

A Decision may select or authorize a Plan and may authorize an Action, but execution remains a separate semantic object.

## Decision context

A Decision should preserve, where applicable:

- decision subject and scope
- decision maker / authority
- alternatives considered
- selected alternative or disposition
- objective references
- constraint and policy references
- evidence and reasoning references
- uncertainty / epistemic status
- scenario or world reference
- decision time
- provenance

## Authority

Decision authority is explicit. A recommendation generated by an AI system, analyst, or optimization engine does not become a Decision merely because it is ranked first. A Decision requires an explicit decision act or authoritative determination.

## Alternatives

The model supports multiple alternatives and a decision may select one, several, or explicitly reject all alternatives. The absence of a selected alternative can itself be a valid disposition.

## Uncertainty and evidence

Decision rationale may reference evidence, diagnosis, causal assessment, scenario evaluation, and reasoning traces. Uncertainty must be preserved rather than converted into certainty by selection.

```text
Evidence / Provenance
        ↓
Evaluation / Diagnosis
        ↓
Recommendation
        ↓
Decision
```

The Decision records the choice; it does not erase the underlying evidence or competing alternatives.

## Temporal semantics

Decision time is distinct from the planned execution time and actual execution time. A later decision does not rewrite the historical decision record.

## Scenario decisions

A decision inside a Scenario or Counterfactual is scoped to that world. It must not be treated as an actual-world decision unless explicitly adopted into the actual world through a separate decision event.

## Reversal and supersession

A later Decision may supersede, amend, or revoke an earlier Decision while preserving the earlier decision as historical truth.

## Non-goals

S139 does not define human approval UI, organizational workflow engines, optimization algorithms, voting systems, or agent implementation. It defines the canonical semantics of a decision independent of those mechanisms.
